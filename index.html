About

Not a series of career changes — twenty-five years spent studying and improving different kinds of systems.

Educated as an information engineer at the University of Novi Sad during a period of deep social and economic disruption, I learned early to analyze systems, adapt quickly, and solve problems with limited resources. Through Lean (PDCA, 5S), I learned to look past symptoms to root causes. Language runs alongside it — C2 English and eleven years teaching professionals taught me to judge the quality of what is said. Today I apply that same judgement to AI: I design, direct and verify intelligent systems rather than write their production code.
